## Why partner with OTCF

OTCF is a registered Nigerian nonprofit that stands behind three active tech communities. Partnering through OTCF means working with a legally recognised organisation while reaching designers and developers through communities they already trust.

## Who you reach

- **Three communities** — Open Designers, its developer community Open Devs, and GDG Ilorin, each with its own members and activities.
- **GDG Ilorin** — {{gdgIlorinMembers}} members in Ilorin, Kwara State.
- **DevFest Ilorin** — the largest tech conference in North Central Nigeria, with {{devfestAttendees}} attendees across past editions. The 2026 edition runs 6–7 November at Ilorin Innovation Hub, with around 1,000 expected each day.

## Ways to work together

- Sponsor an event such as DevFest Ilorin. DevFest Ilorin 2026 is backed by Blaaiz (headline sponsor), Qorelly (Diamond sponsor) and the sponsors and partners listed on /events/devfest-ilorin-2026.
- Support a specific community's programmes.

## How to start

Email OTCF to start a conversation about sponsorship or partnership. For DevFest Ilorin specifically, you can also email GDG Ilorin at gdgilorincmty@gmail.com.
