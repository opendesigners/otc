## What is OTCF?

Open Tech Communities Foundation (OTCF) is a Nigerian nonprofit, registered on 7 May 2026, that is the parent body for three tech communities: GDG Ilorin, Open Designers and its developer community Open Devs. See /about.

## Is OTCF a merger of the communities?

No. OTCF is to its communities what Alphabet is to Google. Each community keeps its own identity, leadership and activities; OTCF provides legal recognition, organisational backing and a shared institutional home.

## Is OTCF registered?

Yes. OTCF was registered on 7 May 2026 as an Incorporated Trustee under CAC Part F. Its registered address is 1 Civil Service Road, GRA, Ilorin, Kwara State, Nigeria.

## Who leads OTCF?

Areous A. Ahmad is the Chairman and founder. Imam Bashir A. and Ahmed Olanrewaju are Co-chairpersons. Faith Ayomide Odetunde is the Secretary and co-trustee.

## Which communities are part of OTCF?

Three: GDG Ilorin (the Google Developer Group chapter in Ilorin), Open Designers (an open-source design and dev community) and Open Devs (Open Designers' developer community). See /communities.

## Where is GDG Ilorin based?

In Ilorin, Kwara State, Nigeria. GDG Ilorin has {{gdgIlorinMembers}} members. See /communities/gdg-ilorin.

## How do I join GDG Ilorin?

Join on the GDG community platform at https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-ilorin/.

## How do I join Open Designers or Open Devs?

Join the Open Designers crew on Discord at https://discord.gg/ZWSCxZcA. Open Devs, its developer community, uses the same Discord. See /communities/open-designers and /communities/open-devs.

## What have OTCF's communities built?

Open Designers has published six free design files on the Figma Community, used {{figmaFileUses}} times, including Headers and Footers and an open-source mobile UI kit. It also built PixelScout, an open-source collection of real app UI screens. GDG Ilorin open-sourced the full design kit from DevFest Ilorin 2025. See /projects.

## What impact have OTCF's communities had?

GDG Ilorin has {{gdgIlorinMembers}} members, Open Designers has {{openDesignersMembers}}, and DevFest Ilorin has welcomed {{devfestAttendees}} attendees across past editions. See /impact.

## When and where is DevFest Ilorin 2026?

6–7 November 2026 at Ilorin Innovation Hub, 9a Ahmadu Bello Way, Ilorin 240212, Kwara. Friday 6 November is workshops; Saturday 7 November is the main conference. See /events/devfest-ilorin-2026.

## How big is DevFest Ilorin?

DevFest Ilorin is the largest tech conference in North Central Nigeria, with {{devfestAttendees}} attendees across past editions. Around 1,000 attendees are expected on each day of the 2026 edition.

## Who sponsors DevFest Ilorin 2026?

Blaaiz is the headline sponsor and Qorelly is the Diamond sponsor. The sponsors are MalHub, Verafy, Ilorin Innovation Hub, SqaleUp and Celerlinks; the partners are Fuskar, Exploit Forge Academy, Pixel Pioneer, StartupList Africa, Unovia and CampusX. See /events/devfest-ilorin-2026.

## Does this website track me?

No. There is no login, no analytics or tracking, and no cookies. Questions typed into the assistant are sent to an AI provider to generate an answer and are not stored by OTCF. See /privacy.
