## What GDG Ilorin is

GDG Ilorin is the Google Developer Group chapter in Ilorin, Kwara State, Nigeria. It has {{gdgIlorinMembers}} members and is one of the three communities under Open Tech Communities Foundation (OTCF). Like every OTCF community, it keeps its own identity, leadership and activities.

## How to join

Join GDG Ilorin on the GDG community platform at https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-ilorin/. Membership is managed there, and upcoming chapter events are listed on the same page.

## DevFest Ilorin

GDG Ilorin runs DevFest Ilorin, the largest tech conference in North Central Nigeria, with {{devfestAttendees}} attendees across past editions. The 2026 edition takes place on 6–7 November 2026 at Ilorin Innovation Hub. See /events/devfest-ilorin-2026.

## Talk recordings

Recordings of GDG Ilorin talks are published on the GDG Ilorin YouTube channel.
