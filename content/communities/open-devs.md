## What Open Devs is

Open Devs is the developer community of Open Designers, and one of the three communities under Open Tech Communities Foundation (OTCF). Design becomes real through development: Open Devs is a home for developers who contribute to open source by building reusable components, improving tooling and powering Figma-to-code workflows. Whether it's React, Web Components or Figma plugins, this is where the code happens.

## How it relates to Open Designers

Open Devs works alongside Open Designers' open-source design files, turning UI kits and design systems into reusable code. The two share one community space. See /communities/open-designers.

## How to join

Join the Open Designers crew on Discord at https://discord.gg/ZWSCxZcA. Open Devs uses the same Discord. The code lives on [GitHub](https://github.com/opendesigners).
