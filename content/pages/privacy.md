## The short version

- **No login.** There are no accounts on this site.
- **No analytics or tracking.** We don't use analytics tools, tracking pixels or advertising scripts.
- **No cookies.** This site doesn't set any.
- **Your questions aren't stored by OTCF.**

## When you ask a question

When you type a question into the box on the home page, it is sent to our server and then to Anthropic, the AI provider that writes the answer. OTCF does not store your question or the answer. Our server logs record only that a request happened, whether it worked and how long it took. They never record what you asked.

Anthropic processes the text of your question under its own data policies. Please don't include personal information, such as your phone number, in a question.

If the AI isn't available, the site shows a list of matching pages from this website instead, and nothing is sent to Anthropic.

## Preventing abuse

To stop the question box being abused, the server briefly counts how many questions come from each network (IP) address. Those counts expire within minutes and are never linked to what was asked.

## Hosting

This site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard technical information, such as IP addresses, to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks.

## Other websites

Pages here link to other sites, such as the GDG community platform. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
