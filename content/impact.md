## Communities people choose to join

GDG Ilorin has grown to {{gdgIlorinMembers}} members on the Google Developer Groups platform, and Open Designers to {{openDesignersMembers}} members. Each community runs its own programmes, with its own leaders. See /communities.

## The biggest tech conference in North Central Nigeria

DevFest Ilorin, run by GDG Ilorin, has welcomed {{devfestAttendees}} attendees across past editions. DevFest Ilorin 2026, on 6–7 November at Ilorin Innovation Hub, is a day of workshops across three halls followed by the main conference, with around 1,000 people expected each day. It is backed by sponsors and partners including Blaaiz (headline sponsor) and Qorelly (Diamond sponsor). See /events/devfest-ilorin-2026.

## Free design files, used tens of thousands of times

Open Designers has published six free design files on the Figma Community under CC BY 4.0. Together they have been used {{figmaFileUses}} times. Headers and Footers alone has 44.9k users. See /projects.

## Given back to other communities

GDG Ilorin open-sourced the complete design system and branding from DevFest Ilorin 2025 (website designs, visual assets, sponsorship and partnership decks, and booth designs) so other tech communities and event organisers can build on it. See /projects.
